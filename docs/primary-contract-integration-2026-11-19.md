# Primary source contract integration — 2026-11-19

This checkpoint integrates Sberbank GoodsCard, Sistematorg section boundaries,
and Centerr UTF-8 decoding on the deployed address-precision baseline. The
original source branches remain in the merge ancestry for review.

## Narrow negative-category recovery

A read-only census identified 2,419 stored property-category rows whose titles
explicitly describe vehicles, equipment, or receivables. These are candidates,
not 2,419 freshly verified source errors. Repair requires a fresh exact primary
publication receipt; cadastral matches and historical database titles alone
cannot authorize an update.

Verified source repair may derive one of those three negative categories from
a freshly observed primary title without claiming that the description was
observed. Both the evidence and repair scopes must include the title, and the
normal full classifier must agree with the explicit title classifier. Positive
property classification still requires fresh title and description. Every
existing identity, source domain, SHA, freshness, exclusion, preview, backup,
and compare-and-set guard remains in force.

Such repairs preserve row IDs, reviews, notes and history. They exclude proven
nonproperty objects from property publication without inventing closed-auction
status. Unobserved descriptions, prices and dates are retained. No physical
deletion is included in this integration.
